Authorize SYNC 24/7

Your Phantom/Solflare owner key never leaves your wallet. This transaction creates or revokes an on-chain policy. SYNC receives only a scoped session key whose permissions are enforced by the policy program. Your Max position amount is moved into your user-owned delegated vault so the executor can trade while your phone and wallet app are closed. A small SOL network reserve is funded to your scoped session signer and pays your own Solana transaction fees and token-account rent. The operator does not subsidize your trades, and unused reserve is returned when you revoke.
